# ClipMotion — Product Hunt launch plan

## Positioning
- **Name:** ClipMotion
- **Tagline (60 characters max):** Paste a link, get a video ad for your product or SaaS
- **Topics:** Marketing, Artificial Intelligence, E-commerce, Video
- **One-line pitch:** ClipMotion reads a product page or a SaaS website and edits a ready-to-post video ad with voice-over, captions and music, in the browser.

## Description (260 characters max)
Paste a product page or your website. ClipMotion writes the script, records the voice-over and edits a video ad with your real product photos, logo and colours. Pick a goal, a format and a length, fine-tune the text and visuals, then download the MP4. Free to try.

## What makes it different (use in the first comment and replies)
1. The ad is built from the **real product photos**; the background is removed, the product is never regenerated.
2. For SaaS, the **real site is demoed** inside a phone and the palette comes from the logo.
3. Goal first: launch, problem → solution, awareness or sales, then one video, not a list of scripts.
4. A small **studio** to rewrite lines, swap a clip or a photo and change the music.
5. Rendering happens in the browser: exact length to the frame, 9:16 / 1:1 / 16:9.

## Maker's first comment (draft)
> Hi Product Hunt! I built ClipMotion because making a video ad for a product page still takes an editor, a script and an afternoon.
> Paste a link: it reads the page (photos, variants, price, logo), you choose what the video is for, and it edits the ad with voice-over, captions and music. You can then rewrite any line or swap a visual.
> Two things I cared about: the product in the video is your real photo, never an AI redraw, and SaaS ads show your actual site.
> It is free to try without an account (3 videos a month, 720p with a watermark). I would love your feedback on the edit quality and on which stores or sites it reads badly.

## Likely questions and honest answers
- *Does it use AI video generation?* No. Scenes are composed and animated from real photos, site captures and stock clips; AI writes the script and can generate a backdrop.
- *Does it work with any store?* It reads Shopify, WooCommerce and pages with structured product data best. Sites protected against automated reading need the photos added by hand.
- *Who owns the video?* The customer. Stock clips come from a royalty-free library and the music is composed by the app.
- *Where is the video rendered?* In the visitor's browser; export time depends on the device.
