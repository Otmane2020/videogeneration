How to make a video ad from a URL in five minutes

If you have a web page, you already have most of a video ad: the product, the photos, the logo, the colours and the message. This guide walks through turning that page into a finished video, and what to check at each step.

In shortPaste your product page or website address into ClipMotion, confirm whether it is an e-commerce or SaaS page, pick the goal, and the tool writes the script, finds the visuals, records the voice-over, adds captions and music, and exports an MP4. The whole process takes a few minutes and needs no filming.

What do you need before you start?

  • The address of one product page (e-commerce) or your home page (SaaS).
  • A clear goal for the ad: launch, awareness, problem and solution, or sales.
  • Five minutes. You do not need an account to try it.

Step 1: Paste the link

Enter the address on the ClipMotion home page. The tool reads the page the way a visitor would: title, headings, price, product photos, logo and brand colours. If the site blocks automated reading, you can add photos by hand in a later step.

Step 2: Confirm the type of page

ClipMotion guesses whether the page sells a physical product or a software service, and you can correct it. The choice matters because the two ads are built differently:

  • E-commerce: the ad is made from your real product photos. The plain background is removed and the product is placed on a new setting, never regenerated.
  • SaaS: the ad combines filmed footage of people in the situation your product solves with a short look at your real website inside a phone frame, plus your logo and colours.

Step 3: Choose the goal and format

Pick what the ad should achieve. A launch ad leads with the product; a problem-and-solution ad opens on the customer's frustration; an awareness ad stresses the brand; a sales ad pushes towards a clear action. Then choose the shape: vertical 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, square 1:1 for feeds, or horizontal 16:9 for YouTube and websites.

Step 4: Let it write and assemble the ad

With one click the tool writes a short script, records the voice-over in the page's language, searches for matching HD footage or prepares your photos, builds word-by-word captions and adds music composed by ClipMotion. A typical result runs about twenty seconds, which suits short-video feeds.

Step 5: Review and adjust

Open the editor to rewrite a line, swap a clip for another from the library, or change the music. Check three things before exporting:

  1. Every claim, price and figure comes from your page. Remove anything you cannot back up.
  2. The product or interface is clearly visible at least once.
  3. The closing screen shows your logo and a button that says what happens next.

Step 6: Export

Download the MP4. The free preview carries a small watermark; the clean export is sold per video or through a subscription. Exports in 720p and 1080p are available, and you can reuse the same ad in the other formats.

Which pages work best?

  • Product pages with clear photos on a plain background give the cleanest cut-outs.
  • SaaS home pages with a clear headline and a visible logo give the strongest brand match.
  • Pages that require a login or block bots work better with photos added by hand.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to make a video ad from a URL?

A few minutes. You paste the link, choose a goal and a format, and the tool generates the script, voice-over, captions, music and edit.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can paste a link and get a first ad right away. An account is only needed to keep your history and subscribe.

Can I change the script after it is generated?

Yes. You can rewrite any line, swap a clip and change the music, then regenerate the video.

What file do I get?

An MP4 you can upload to Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, ad managers or your website.
