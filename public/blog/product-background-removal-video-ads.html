How to Remove Product Backgrounds for Video Ads

ClipMotion is an AI video ad generator that turns SaaS and app landing pages or e-commerce product pages into short video ads with scripts, voice-over, captions, and music.

Can an AI tool remove a product background and stage the item in an ad?

Yes. ClipMotion can separate a product from its photo background and place the cutout on a studio backdrop. Start with a clear photo of the exact variant you are advertising.

How to prepare and check a product cutout

  1. Choose a well-lit image with the whole product in frame.
  2. Check the cutout at full size, especially glass, straps, shadows and open spaces.
  3. Choose a backdrop with enough contrast around the edges and leave room for captions.
  4. Compare the final frame with the source photo. Check the colour, logo, shape and variant before publishing.

If an edge looks wrong, try a clearer photo or use the original photo in that scene. See the product showcase workflow for the rest of the ad.
