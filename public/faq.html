ClipMotion FAQ

ClipMotion is an AI video ad generator that turns SaaS and app landing pages or e-commerce product pages into short video ads with scripts, voice-over, captions, and music.

What can I start with?

Use a public SaaS or app landing page or an e-commerce product page. If a store page cannot be read, add product photos manually.

What does ClipMotion generate?

A draft video ad with a script, voice-over, captions and music. Review the script and product visuals in the browser before exporting.

What formats are available?

MP4 in vertical 9:16, square 1:1 or horizontal 16:9. See pricing and export options and the format guide.
