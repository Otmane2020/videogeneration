FAQ

Before you start

Do I need an account?

No. Paste a link and your first ad is edited right away, in your browser.

Does the AI change my product photos?

No. Only the plain background is removed. The product is never regenerated: its shape, logo and colours stay exactly as photographed.

What if my site blocks automated reading?

Add your photos by hand in the next step and the ad is built from them.

Where do the footage and music come from?

Stock footage comes from Pixabay, under its licence. The music is composed by the app, with no rights to clear.
